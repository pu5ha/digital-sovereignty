## At a glance

|Project|Main strength|Main limitation|
|---|---|---|
|[[Tor]]|Most mature anonymity network with broad adoption|Vulnerable to timing analysis and relatively slow|
|[[I2P (Invisible Internet Project)]]|Excellent for anonymous peer-to-peer and internal services|Less suited for accessing the public Internet|
|[[Nym]]|Strongest protection against metadata analysis through mixnets and cover traffic|Higher latency than traditional anonymity networks|
|[[Lokinet]]|Fast anonymous networking with onion routing|Weaker metadata protection than a mixnet|
|[[HOPR]]|Privacy-preserving transport layer for decentralized applications|Not designed as a full anonymous browsing network|
