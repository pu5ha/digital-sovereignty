## What is it?

The Session Technology Foundation, often abbreviated as **STF**, is a Swiss nonprofit foundation dedicated to promoting digital innovation and digital rights.

Its main responsibility is stewarding the [[Session]] private messaging ecosystem. This includes contributing to Session’s software, protocol, network infrastructure, education, advocacy, and broader development.

The foundation describes its guiding principles as:

- Robustness through decentralization
    
- Privacy through encryption
    
- Choice through interoperability

The Session Technology Foundation officially took over stewardship of Session on **October 11, 2024**.

## Legal structure

The Session Technology Foundation is legally established as a **Swiss foundation**.

Unlike a traditional company, it does not have shareholders or owners entitled to profits. Under the Swiss foundation structure, the organization is legally bound to pursue the purpose defined in its founding documents.

Its stated purpose is to promote digital innovation and digital rights.

## Relationship to Session

The foundation does not personally operate every part of Session’s network.

Its role includes:

- Coordinating development of the Session applications
    
- Contributing to the Session protocol
    
- Supporting node and blockchain software
    
- Funding essential network infrastructure
    
- Educating users about privacy
    
- Advocating for digital rights
    
- Supporting grants and ecosystem development

The actual messaging network is distributed across independently operated nodes. The foundation acts as the main organizational steward, while the infrastructure itself is intended to remain decentralized.

## Funding

The foundation is funded through a combination of:

- Individual donations
    
- Cryptocurrency donations
    
- Community fundraising
    
- Resources associated with the Session ecosystem

It does not rely on advertising or selling user data.

In early 2026, financial constraints forced the foundation to pause Session development. A subsequent donation campaign raised enough money for development to continue in a more limited capacity, although the foundation has continued seeking additional funding. Its public fundraising campaign set a goal of **$1 million**.
## Business model

The foundation is not intended to operate like a conventional profit-seeking technology company.

Session is free to use, and the foundation does not fund it through:

- Advertisements
    
- Selling personal data
    
- Paid premium messaging features
    
- Shareholder investment

Instead, it depends heavily on donations and ecosystem resources to pay developers, support infrastructure, and continue protocol development.

## Open-source contributions

The foundation has committed to keeping the Session ecosystem open source.

This commitment covers foundation-operated or administered contributions to:

- Session mobile clients
    
- Session Desktop
    
- The Session messaging protocol
    
- Node software
    
- Core blockchain components
    
- Supporting infrastructure

The foundation says this commitment is intended to keep Session auditable, interoperable, and resistant to control by a single organization.
