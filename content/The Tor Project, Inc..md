## How it is funded

The Tor Project is funded through a mixture of:

- Government grants, including funding from US agencies
    
- Private foundations
    
- Companies and nonprofit organizations
    
- Individual donations
    
- Membership and sponsorship programs
    

In its 2023–2024 fiscal year, individual donors contributed about $1.1 million, representing roughly 15.6% of total revenue.

### Financial position

As of **June 30, 2024** (its latest audited financial statements):

- **Total assets:** **$6.96 million**
- **Cash and cash equivalents:** **$5.68 million**
- **Net assets (essentially the nonprofit's equity):** **$6.67 million**
- **Annual revenue:** **$7.29 million** (IRS Form 990) or **$8.01 million** when including in-kind donations in the audited financial statements.

### Employees

The Tor Project has **around 40 full-time staff** spread across engineering, research, UX, anti-censorship, operations, fundraising, legal, communications, and administration. They are a fully remote organization with employees around the world.
