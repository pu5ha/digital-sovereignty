## What is it?

IVPN is a privacy-focused VPN service that encrypts your Internet traffic and routes it through IVPN's servers, hiding your IP address from websites, your ISP, and other network observers.

Founded in 2009, IVPN is known for taking a minimalist, privacy-first approach. Rather than offering the largest server network or the most features, it focuses on transparency, strong security, and collecting as little user information as possible.

## Who maintains it?

IVPN is operated by [[IVPN Limited (formerly Privatus Limited)]], a company headquartered in **Gibraltar**.

## Cost to use?

IVPN offers two paid plans:

- **IVPN Standard**
- **IVPN Pro**

There is no free tier.

## Is it a public good?

No.

IVPN is a commercial VPN provider.

However, it has earned a strong reputation within the privacy community for its transparency, open-source software, and commitment to minimizing user data collection.

## How it works

Like other VPNs, IVPN creates an encrypted connection between your device and one of its VPN servers.

When you connect:

1. Your device establishes an encrypted connection using WireGuard or OpenVPN.
2. All of your Internet traffic is securely sent to the IVPN server.
3. The VPN server decrypts the traffic and forwards it to its final destination.
4. Websites see the VPN server's IP address instead of your own.

```text
You
   ↓
Encrypted Connection
   ↓
IVPN Server
   ↓
Website
```

Your ISP can see that you're connected to IVPN but cannot see which websites you're visiting.

## What makes it different?

IVPN emphasizes simplicity and transparency over feature count.

Some of its distinguishing characteristics include:

- No email required to create an account
- Anonymous account IDs
- Cash and Monero payments
- Fully open-source applications
- RAM-only servers
- Regular independent security audits
- Clear, easy-to-understand privacy policies
- Minimal tracking on its own website

## Main limitations

- Smaller server network than many competitors.
- No free plan.
- Fewer features aimed at streaming or bypassing geo-restrictions.
- Like any centralized VPN, using IVPN requires trusting the company to operate its infrastructure honestly.
