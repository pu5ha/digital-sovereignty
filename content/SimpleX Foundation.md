## What is it?

The SimpleX Foundation is a nonprofit organization established to steward the **[[SimpleX Chat]]** ecosystem and the broader **SimpleX Network**.

Its mission is to ensure that the SimpleX protocols remain open, interoperable, and governed in the interests of users rather than any single company. The foundation participates in the governance of the SimpleX Network and supports the long-term development of privacy-preserving communication technologies.

## Legal structure

The SimpleX Foundation is a **nonprofit foundation** incorporated in **Delaware, United States**.

It was established in **2026** and is pursuing U.S. **501(c)(3)** tax-exempt status.

- **Matt Odell** — Co-founder of OpenSats and privacy advocate
    
- **Tadeáš Kmenta** — Co-founder of Flux
    
- **Evgeny Poberezkin** — Founder of SimpleX Chat and creator of the SimpleX Network protocols

## Relationship to SimpleX

The foundation acts as the long-term steward of the SimpleX Network.

Its responsibilities include:

- Participating in protocol governance
    
- Protecting the SimpleX Network specifications
    
- Maintaining open-source licensing
    
- Supporting interoperability
    
- Ensuring no single organization controls the protocol

Day-to-day software development is carried out by the parties to the **SimpleX Network Consortium**, while the foundation helps ensure the protocol continues to evolve for the benefit of users.

## Funding

The foundation is a nonprofit organization and is expected to be funded through:

- Individual donations
    
- Grants
    
- Community support
    
- Contributions from organizations participating in the SimpleX ecosystem

It does not rely on advertising or selling user data.

## Business model

The foundation is not a profit-seeking company.

Its purpose is to steward the SimpleX Network and support the long-term development of privacy-preserving communication infrastructure.

SimpleX Chat remains free to use, and the foundation does not monetize users through:

- Advertisements
    
- Selling personal data
    
- Subscription messaging features

## Philosophy

The SimpleX Foundation believes private communication should not require trusted intermediaries or permanent digital identities.

Its philosophy emphasizes:

- Privacy by default
    
- Open-source development
    
- Interoperability
    
- Decentralized governance
    
- Minimal metadata collection
    
- User ownership
    
- Protocol neutrality

## Open-source contributions

The foundation supports the continued development of the open-source SimpleX ecosystem, including:

- SimpleX Chat
    
- SimpleX Network protocols
    
- Relay server software
    
- Protocol specifications
    
- Open governance of the network

The foundation also participates in governing changes to the protocol through the **SimpleX Network Consortium**, alongside other participating organizations.

## Main limitations

- The foundation is very new, having been established in 2026.
    
- Governance is still evolving as the SimpleX ecosystem grows.
    
- Long-term sustainability depends on continued community funding and participation.
    
- While the protocol is openly governed, much of the core development is still led by a relatively small group of contributors.
