## What is it?

The Signal Foundation is a nonprofit organization dedicated to developing open-source technology for secure and private communication.

Founded in 2018, its mission is to make private communication accessible to everyone by developing Signal and the underlying Signal Protocol without relying on advertising, venture capital, or selling user data.

The foundation owns **Signal Messenger LLC**, the company responsible for developing and operating the Signal messaging service.

## Who maintains it?

The Signal Foundation is an independent nonprofit organization based in **California, United States**.

It was founded by:

- **Moxie Marlinspike**
    
- **Brian Acton** (co-founder of WhatsApp)
    

## Leadership

- **Meredith Whittaker** — President
    
- **Jun Harada** — Chief Executive Officer
    
- **Brian Acton** — Executive Chairman
    

## Team

The Signal Foundation employs approximately **100–150 people**, including engineers, cryptographers, designers, infrastructure engineers, support staff, and operations personnel.

The majority of the team focuses on developing and maintaining Signal and the Signal Protocol.

## Funding

The Signal Foundation is primarily funded through:

- Individual donations
    
- Grants
    
- A **$50 million** loan from Brian Acton when the foundation was established
    

Unlike most large messaging platforms, Signal has not raised venture capital and does not generate revenue through advertising or selling user data.

## Business model

Signal is free to use.

The foundation supports development through:

- Individual donations
    
- Monthly sustaining members
    
- Grants
    
- Philanthropic contributions
    

There are:

- No advertisements
    
- No subscription tiers
    
- No sale of user data
    

## Philosophy

The Signal Foundation believes private communication is a fundamental human right.

Its guiding principles include:

- End-to-end encryption by default
    
- Collect as little user data as possible
    
- Open-source development
    
- Strong cryptography
    
- Transparency
    
- No advertising-based incentives
    
- Technology that serves users rather than advertisers
    

## Open-source contributions

The Signal Foundation develops and maintains several widely used open-source projects, including:

- Signal Messenger
    
- Signal Desktop
    
- Signal Server
    
- Signal Protocol
    
- Supporting cryptographic libraries
    

The Signal Protocol has become the industry standard for end-to-end encrypted messaging and has been adopted by numerous other applications.

## Main limitations

- Operates centralized infrastructure for message delivery.
    
- Requires ongoing donations to fund development and operations.
    
- Signal's privacy guarantees depend on widespread adoption of the application and protocol.
    
- While the software is open source, the service is operated by a single nonprofit organization.
