## What is it?

IVPN Limited is the company that develops and operates the IVPN VPN service. Founded in 2009, the company's mission is to provide privacy-first Internet access through transparent engineering, strong security, and minimal data collection.

Unlike many VPN companies that emphasize streaming or large server networks, IVPN focuses almost exclusively on privacy and security.

The company operated under the name **Privatus Limited** until 2023, when it changed its legal name to **IVPN Limited** to better align with its public brand. 

## Who maintains it?

IVPN Limited is a privately held company headquartered in Gibraltar.

It has no parent company and is independently owned. 

## Leadership

- **Nicholas Pestell** — Founder & CEO

Pestell is also the company's sole owner. He founded IVPN after studying Information Security at the University of London (Royal Holloway).

## Funding

IVPN is privately owned and bootstrapped.

The company has not raised venture capital and is funded entirely through subscriptions to its VPN service.

## Business model

IVPN generates revenue through paid VPN subscriptions.

Key characteristics include:

- No free tier
- No advertising
- No affiliate marketing
- No selling of customer data
- Subscription-funded business

## Philosophy

IVPN believes privacy is a prerequisite for personal autonomy and that strong encryption should be widely available.

Its guiding principles include:

- Collect as little user information as possible
- Build open-source software
- Publish independent security audits
- Operate transparently
- Resist unnecessary surveillance
- Keep products simple and security-focused

## Open-source contributions

IVPN develops and maintains several open-source projects, including:

- IVPN desktop applications
- IVPN mobile applications
- Supporting networking and security tools

The company also regularly commissions independent security audits and publishes the results publicly.

## Main limitations

- IVPN remains a centralized company operating its own VPN infrastructure.
- The server infrastructure is not open source.
- Compared to larger competitors, IVPN has a relatively small team and server network.
- As with any VPN provider, users must trust IVPN to operate its infrastructure according to its stated privacy policies.
