## What is it?

NordVPN is a commercial VPN service that encrypts your Internet traffic and routes it through NordVPN's global network of VPN servers, hiding your IP address from websites, your ISP, and other network observers.

Launched in 2012, NordVPN has become one of the world's largest VPN providers, serving millions of users across more than 100 countries. In addition to privacy, it emphasizes performance, ease of use, and cybersecurity features.

## Who maintains it?

NordVPN is developed and operated by [[Nord Security]], a cybersecurity company headquartered in **Vilnius, Lithuania**.

## Cost to use?

NordVPN offers several subscription tiers:

- Plus

Plans are available monthly, annually, and every two years.

There is no free tier.

## Is it a public good?

No.

NordVPN is a commercial VPN service operated by Nord Security. Its primary goal is to provide privacy and cybersecurity services through paid subscriptions.

## How it works

Like other VPNs, NordVPN creates an encrypted connection between your device and one of its VPN servers.

When you connect:

1. Your device establishes an encrypted connection using NordLynx (WireGuard-based), WireGuard, or OpenVPN.
2. All of your Internet traffic is securely sent to the NordVPN server.
3. The VPN server decrypts the traffic and forwards it to its final destination.
4. Websites see the VPN server's IP address instead of your own.

```text
You
   ↓
Encrypted Connection
   ↓
NordVPN Server
   ↓
Website
```

Your ISP can see that you're connected to NordVPN but cannot see which websites you're visiting.

## What makes it different?

NordVPN focuses on combining privacy with performance and security.

Some of its distinguishing features include:

- NordLynx, a WireGuard-based protocol optimized for speed and privacy
- One of the world's largest VPN server networks
- Double VPN (multi-hop routing)
- Onion Over VPN servers for accessing the Tor network through NordVPN
- Obfuscated servers for bypassing censorship
- Threat Protection features that block malware, trackers, and malicious websites
- Independently audited no-logs policy

## Main limitations

- Requires an email address to create an account.
- Client applications are not open source.
- The service is built around a proprietary ecosystem with many premium features beyond the VPN itself.
- Like any centralized VPN, using NordVPN requires trusting the company to operate its infrastructure according to its stated privacy policy.
