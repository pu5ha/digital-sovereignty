# Nym Technologies

## What is it?

Nym Technologies SA is a Swiss company founded in 2018 that develops privacy infrastructure focused on protecting internet metadata.

Its mission is to build privacy-preserving networking technology that can resist mass surveillance and traffic analysis.

## Headquarters

Neuchâtel, Switzerland.

## What does the company build?

Nym Technologies develops:

- [[Nym]]
- [[NymVPN]]
- SDKs for developers
- Nym Wallet
- Software for [[Mixnets]], gateways, and node operators

The company develops the software, but the network itself is operated by independent node operators around the world.

## Founders

- [[Harry Halpin]] — CEO

The founding team combines expertise in cryptography, distributed systems, privacy research, and anonymous communication.

## Funding

Nym has raised approximately **$52 million** from investors.

Notable investors include:

- Andreessen Horowitz (a16z)
- Binance Labs
- Polychain Capital
- Eden Block
- European Commission

Unlike [[The Tor Project, Inc.]], Nym is a venture-backed company rather than a nonprofit.

## Business model

Nym develops open-source software, but its long-term business model is built around the [[NYM Token]].

Applications pay to use the network, and those fees reward independent node operators that provide bandwidth and privacy.

Rather than relying on donations and volunteers like [[Tor]], Nym aims to make privacy infrastructure economically sustainable through crypto-economic incentives.
