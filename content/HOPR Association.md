## What is it?

The HOPR Association is a Swiss nonprofit association founded in 2020 to develop, fund, and steward the HOPR protocol.

Its purpose is to promote privacy-preserving communication technologies and protect personal data from surveillance and exploitation.

Unlike many crypto projects, HOPR is not owned or operated by a traditional corporation.

## Headquarters

Zurich, Switzerland.

## What does it do?

The Association is responsible for:

- Developing the HOPR protocol
- Funding research and engineering
- Managing the protocol treasury
- Supporting ecosystem growth
- Overseeing governance
- Maintaining the open-source project

The decentralized network itself is operated by independent node operators around the world.

## Why a Swiss Association?

Switzerland has well-established legal structures for nonprofit associations and blockchain organizations.

The Association provides a legal entity that can:

- Hire employees
- Own intellectual property
- Receive grants and donations
- Sign contracts
- Interact with governments and companies

while working toward progressively decentralizing governance.

## Governance

HOPR's long-term goal is **community governance**.

The project describes its model as **Decentralized Community-Enabling Governance (DecenGov)**.

Rather than immediately becoming a DAO, the Association serves as a legal wrapper that can gradually hand decision-making to the community while still allowing the project to operate in the real world.

Holders of the HOPR token are intended to participate in governance and, over time, become members of the Association with voting rights.

## Funding

The HOPR Association has been funded through:

- Venture investment (including Binance Labs' early investment)
- Token sales
- Treasury assets
- Grants

Unlike [[The Tor Project]], it is **not** primarily donation-funded.
