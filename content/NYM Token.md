## What is it?

The NYM token is the native utility token of the Nym ecosystem.

It is primarily used to coordinate and economically incentivize the [[Nym]] network rather than to provide privacy itself.

Unlike an ERC-20 token, the native NYM token lives on the **Nyx** blockchain, Nym's own Layer 1 built using the Cosmos SDK.

An ERC-20 version of NYM also exists on Ethereum for trading and DeFi, but node operators must use **native NYM** on the Nyx chain to participate in the network.

## Why does it exist?

The token allows the network to operate without relying on a centralized company to collect payments and pay node operators.

It is used to:

- Bond (stake) mix nodes
- Delegate stake to node operators
- Reward node operators
- Pay for network usage
- Participate in governance

## What is the Nyx chain?

The Nyx chain is Nym's own Layer 1 blockchain.

It does **not** route internet traffic.

Instead, it keeps track of:

- NYM token ownership
- Node registration
- Bonding and delegation
- Reward distribution
- Governance

The actual privacy network is the [[Nym]] [[Mixnets|mixnet]]. The Nyx chain simply coordinates its economics.

## How staking works

There are two different types of participants:

### Mix node operators

Operators bond NYM tokens and run mix nodes that forward encrypted traffic through the network.

They earn NYM rewards based on factors such as:

- Amount bonded
- Delegated stake
- Performance
- Uptime

### Delegators

Users who do not want to run a node can delegate their NYM to an operator.

Delegators share in the operator's rewards while helping trustworthy nodes attract more stake.

Unlike Ethereum, mix node operators are **not** securing blockchain consensus. They are providing privacy infrastructure.

## Token distribution

Total supply: **1,000,000,000 NYM**

Initial allocation:

- ~25% — Mix mining rewards (node operators)
- ~20% — Backers and investors
- ~20% — Reserve / treasury
- ~15% — Team
- ~10% — Community incentives
- ~10% — Advisors and strategic contributors

The distribution was designed to fund long-term network operation while rewarding the people who provide privacy infrastructure.
