## What is it?

Mullvad VPN AB is the Swedish company that develops and operates the Mullvad VPN service. Founded in 2009, its mission is to protect the universal right to privacy by providing a VPN that minimizes the amount of trust users must place in the provider.

The company is best known for taking an engineering-first approach to privacy, prioritizing anonymity, transparency, and open-source software over aggressive marketing or growth.

## Who maintains it?

Mullvad VPN AB is a subsidiary of **Amagicom AB**.

The company is 100% owned by its founders:

- Fredrik Strömberg
- Daniel Berntsson

Both founders remain actively involved in the company. 
## Leadership

The company is led by its two founders, who serve as co-CEOs:

- Fredrik Strömberg
- Daniel Berntsson

## Team

Approximately **55 employees**.

Unlike many companies, Mullvad publicly lists every member of its team on its website, reflecting its emphasis on transparency.

## Funding

Mullvad is privately owned and bootstrapped.

The company does not rely on venture capital or outside investors. Its revenue comes from subscriptions to its VPN service.

## Business model

Mullvad earns revenue through its VPN subscriptions.

Key aspects of its model include:

- Flat €5 monthly subscription
- No free tier
- No advertising
- No affiliate marketing or sponsored reviews
- No data collection or monetization of user information
## Open-source contributions

Beyond its VPN service, Mullvad develops and contributes to several privacy tools, including:

- Mullvad Browser (developed with the Tor Project)
- Open-source VPN clients
- Privacy-focused networking tools
- Contributions to WireGuard and other open-source software projects
