## At a glance

|VPN|Main strength|Main limitation|
|---|---|---|
|[[Mullvad]]|Anonymous accounts, cash payments, and a strong privacy-first design|Still requires trusting one centralized VPN provider|
|[[Proton VPN]]|Strong overall privacy ecosystem and a usable free plan|Requires a Proton account|
|[[IVPN]]|Transparency, minimal data collection, and anonymous account IDs|Smaller server network and fewer mainstream features|
|[[NordVPN]]|Large server network, strong performance, and censorship-bypassing tools|Proprietary apps and less anonymous account creation|
|[[NymVPN]]|Decentralized routing and protection against network metadata analysis|Newer network with slower performance in its strongest privacy mode|
