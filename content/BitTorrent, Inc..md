## What was it?

BitTorrent, Inc. was a private software company founded to build products and businesses around the [[BitTorrent]] protocol.

It was founded in **2004** by [[Bram Cohen]], the protocol’s creator, and [[Ashwin Navin]].

The company developed official torrent applications, acquired [[µTorrent]], and attempted to turn BitTorrent’s enormous user base into a sustainable commercial business.

## BitTorrent protocol vs company

These are separate:

|Name|Meaning|
|---|---|
|[[BitTorrent]]|The open peer-to-peer file-distribution protocol|
|BitTorrent client|Software that uses the protocol|
|BitTorrent, Inc.|The company that developed official BitTorrent products|
|[[µTorrent]]|A popular proprietary BitTorrent client acquired by the company|

The company could control its own applications and trademarks, but it could not control the entire protocol or prevent independent developers from creating compatible clients.

## Products

Its best-known products included:

- **BitTorrent Mainline** — The original official BitTorrent client
    
- **[[µTorrent]]** — A lightweight torrent client acquired in 2006
    
- **BitTorrent Web** — A browser-based torrent client
    
- **BitTorrent Android** — A mobile torrent client
    
- **Premium and advertisement-free versions** of its software

The company’s current consumer business still markets BitTorrent and µTorrent applications across desktop, web, and Android.

## Business model

BitTorrent became an extremely successful protocol, but the company repeatedly struggled to build a strong business around it.

It experimented with:

- Advertising inside torrent clients
    
- Paid, advertisement-free software
    
- Media distribution partnerships
    
- A licensed video store
    
- Content-delivery services
    
- File synchronization
    
- Premium security and VPN bundles

Many attempts failed because the protocol was open, alternative clients were widely available, and BitTorrent was strongly associated with unauthorized file sharing.

## Acquisition of µTorrent

BitTorrent, Inc. acquired µTorrent in **2006**.

µTorrent was already popular because it was lightweight and efficient. Its code later became the foundation of the official BitTorrent client, meaning the BitTorrent and µTorrent applications became closely related products.

## Acquisition by TRON

In **2018**, the company and its products were acquired by [[TRON]], the blockchain ecosystem founded by [[Justin Sun]].

The reported acquisition price was approximately **$140 million**. Bram Cohen left the company around the time of the acquisition and later focused on [[Chia Network]].

The legal company was subsequently known as **Rainberry, Inc.**, although its consumer products continued using the BitTorrent brand.

## Relationship to crypto

After the acquisition, TRON introduced crypto-related products around the BitTorrent brand.

These included:

- **BTT** — The BitTorrent ecosystem token
    
- **BitTorrent Speed** — Intended to reward users for providing bandwidth
    
- **BitTorrent Chain** — A blockchain interoperability network

The goal was to add financial incentives to an ecosystem that had traditionally depended on voluntary file sharing.

These crypto systems are separate from the original BitTorrent protocol. A person can use ordinary BitTorrent without BTT, TRON, or any blockchain.

## What is it today?

Today, the BitTorrent brand remains primarily associated with consumer torrent software such as:

- BitTorrent Web
    
- BitTorrent Classic
    
- BitTorrent Android
    
- µTorrent
    
- Newer macOS clients

It is now part of the wider TRON ecosystem rather than the independent company originally led by Bram Cohen.

## Main limitations

- The company never controlled the open BitTorrent protocol.
    
- It struggled for years to monetize an extremely popular technology.
    
- Its official clients became proprietary and advertisement-supported.
    
- Independent clients often compete directly with its products.
    
- The BitTorrent brand remains strongly associated with copyright infringement.
    
- Its later crypto products are conceptually separate from the protocol that made the company famous.
    
- The relationship among the BitTorrent brand, Rainberry, TRON, BTT, and BitTorrent Chain can be confusing.

## Key takeaway

> BitTorrent, Inc. was the company created to commercialize the BitTorrent protocol. It acquired µTorrent, struggled to find a sustainable business model, and was acquired by TRON in 2018. The open BitTorrent protocol continues to exist independently of the company and its crypto products.
